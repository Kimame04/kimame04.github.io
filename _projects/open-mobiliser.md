An assistive mobility technology solution created for the **Tech For Good Festival 2021** organized by Open Enablers.

## Project Scope

The project focused on designing accessible digital tools for individuals with mobility challenges:

- Streamlined interface designed for low-friction navigation.
- Accessible information architecture for community support and equipment sharing.
